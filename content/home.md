## About Region 13

AYSO Region 13 has served families in the Pasadena area since 1969. Our volunteer-run organization provides youth soccer based on AYSO's core philosophies: Everyone Plays, Balanced Teams, Open Registration, Positive Coaching, and Good Sportsmanship.
