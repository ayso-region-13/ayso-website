# Oak Grove

Oak Grove is a practice field in La Cañada.

---

## Location

4463 Oak Grove Dr, La Cañada CA 91011

[INLEAGUE: Google Maps link]

---

## Parking

Park in the LCHS student lot, accessed from Foothill Boulevard (use the turn closest to Oak Grove Avenue).

Do not enter the lot adjacent to St. Francis field.
