# Women's Coaching Alliance

The Women's Coaching Alliance (WCA) partners with Region 13 to develop young women as sports coaches and leaders.

---

## Why It Matters

About 94% of women in executive positions have a background in sports. Coaching provides leadership skills that benefit careers beyond athletics.

---

## For Young Coaches (Ages 15-26)

The program pairs young women with AYSO teams:
- Work in pairs to coach teams
- Receive guidance from two adult mentors
- Attend leadership and coaching classes
- Receive compensation for coaching

### How to Apply
Visit [womenscoachingalliance.org/coaching/become-a-coach](https://womenscoachingalliance.org/coaching/become-a-coach)

---

## For Adult Mentors

Mentors support young coaches through the season. Both experienced and non-experienced mentors are welcome.

### Time Commitment
- 30 minutes to 3 hours weekly during season
- Check-in meetings
- Attendance at practices and games

The approach emphasizes hands-on learning — mentors let young coaches develop problem-solving skills with support rather than real-time intervention.

### How to Sign Up
Visit [womenscoachingalliance.org/take-action/mentor](https://womenscoachingalliance.org/take-action/mentor)
