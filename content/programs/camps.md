# Summer Camps

Region 13 does not run summer camps directly, but several organizations offer soccer and multi-sport camps in our area.

## Soccer Camps

### Launch Sports
Soccer and multi-sport camps throughout spring break and summer.
- Website: [launchsports.org](https://launchsports.org)

### UK International Soccer
Soccer instruction sponsored by AYSO.
- 2025 dates: June 23, July 14, July 28
- Location: Near the Zoo
- Website: [uksoccer.com](https://uksoccer.com)

### Legacy FC
- 2025 dates: June 23 and July 21
- Location: Victory Park
- Free for low-income and fire-affected families
- Website: [lgcyfc.com/camps](https://lgcyfc.com/camps)

### Challenger Sports
- 2025 dates: June 9-13
- Location: Near Kidspace
- Website: [challengersports.com](https://challengersports.com)

### St. Francis High School
- 2025 dates: Week of July 28
- Website: [campbrainregistration.com](https://campbrainregistration.com)

## Multi-Sport Camps

### Spartan All-Stars
Multi-sport focus for various ages.

### Coach Andy's Assist Athletics
Multi-sport camps for ages 4-12.
- Location: La Cañada
- Website: [assistathletics.com](https://assistathletics.com)
