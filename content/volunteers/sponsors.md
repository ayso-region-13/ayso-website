# Sponsorship Opportunities

Support youth soccer through sponsorship. All donations are tax-deductible.

---

## Sponsorship Levels

### Child Athlete Sponsorship
Any contribution supports equipment replacement and assists children with financial need. The region typically supports 200-250 kids annually who need financial assistance.

### Team Sponsor — $250
Your name and logo appear on the team banner, displayed at games throughout the season. This level covers uniforms and equipment costs. Teams cover banner production expenses. Sponsors may designate a specific team.

### Field Sponsor — $500+
Recognition on Victory Park banners, season t-shirts, and newsletter promotion. Funds field maintenance, lighting, and equipment.

### League Sponsor — $5,000
Supports major infrastructure investments including new goals, field-painting equipment, and clubhouse improvements.
