# Volunteering with Region 13

AYSO is an all-volunteer organization. Every team needs five key volunteers: two coaches, two referees, and one team manager.

---

## Why Volunteer

Volunteers make youth soccer possible. AYSO has only 18 paid staff members nationwide; everything else is done by parent volunteers like you.

When you volunteer, you:
- Support your child's soccer experience directly
- Join a community of families working toward a common goal
- Develop new skills
- Shape how kids learn sportsmanship and teamwork

---

## Volunteer Requirements

All volunteers must complete:

1. **Volunteer Application** — Through InLeague
2. **Background Check** — Free, through Sterling Volunteers
3. **LiveScan Fingerprinting** — One-time, at Only Stop Travel on Woodbury Road (first-time volunteers only)
4. **Safety Training** — CDC Concussion Awareness, Sudden Cardiac Arrest Awareness, AYSO Safe Haven
5. **SafeSport Training** — Annual renewal required

## Role-Specific Training

Beyond the universal requirements, each role has additional training:

### Coaches
Age-specific certification course (6U, 8U, 10U, 12U, or advanced). See [Coach Training](/coaches/training).

### Referees
8U Official course or Regional Referee course depending on division. See [Referee Training](/referees/training).

### Team Managers
Orientation video, Safe Haven training, and background check. See [Manager Training](/managers/training).

### 4U/5U Volunteers
No additional training required. The league hires professional coaches for these youngest divisions.
