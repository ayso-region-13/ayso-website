This Week is Silent Saturday
As we head into week 3 of games, it’s time to refocus on what makes AYSO Soccer unlike any other youth sports program. 

Silent Saturday, when coaches and parents are asked not to instruct or cheer verbally, gives kids a chance to play a purer form of soccer, free from coaches’ and spectators’ direction from the sidelines. 

The reasons we do this are: 
  • To emphasize that the game is about letting the kids have fun and play.
  • To show that kids can play well on their own with limited instruction.
  • To help the few parents and coaches who feel they must provide constant direction understand how disruptive it can be.
  • To give players the chance to trust their skills and instincts without sideline input.
  • To encourage leadership skills among players
  • To encourage a sense of true teamwork as players must rely upon one another
  • To support our volunteer referees by eliminating sideline interference and comments.
While coaches and family members are asked to remain silent… clapping is allowed! Be creative in how you choose to cheer your child’s team. There are lots of ways to cheer other than verbally; applaud/clap, but please no horns, whistles, or noise-makers. We’ll be hosting a special sign-making station at Victory Park to help soccer families show their silent appreciation for all the players doing their best on the fields.
Reminder: NO DOGS & NO PETS at the fields
We love all furry friends, but dogs & other pets are not allowed on the sidelines at games, or practices. Thanks for helping to keep all players and spectators safe!

Letter from the Commissioner

Hello everyone,

I spent time last Saturday visiting our fields, observing our community enjoying soccer together in the beautiful Southern California weather. As the day went on, I was especially touched by one group of youth about whom many may be unaware: our youth referees. Every year the Referee Staff trains a couple dozen of our youth as referees - for both the noncompetitive younger divisions and the older competitive divisions. We have youth serving as Referees in every division from 6U to 14U. I am always impressed to see someone who has played in AYSO but who is now excited to give back to the game as a Referee. These youth have a wealth of experience actually playing the game of soccer, so often they are very skilled at the Laws of the Game and recognizing how they work in practice.

If you know of a youth interested in refereeing, have them send us an e-mail to proref@ayso13.org. (PRO stands for Player Referee Organization.) We support them with training, customized equipment, and special activities.
And if you have a youth serving as your Referee this weekend - or even one of your Coaches - let’s go out of our way this week to thank them.

—Patrick Shopbell, your volunteer RC
