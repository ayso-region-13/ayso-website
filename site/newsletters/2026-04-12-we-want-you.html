Fall Signups & Updated Age Groups Coming Soon! 


Registration for the Fall 2026 season opens soon!

What’s New in the Fall of 2026: The cutoff date for player divisions has moved from December 31 to August 1. This change was instituted by AYSO, US Youth Soccer, and US Club Soccer in the Summer of 2025.


This affects players born between August 1–December 31. For the upcoming Fall 2026 season, players with birthdays in this date range will remain in the same division they were in during the Fall 2025 season. This will result in approximately 40% of players in Region 13 playing an additional year in a specific division. For example, players born between Aug 1 and Dec 31 may spend two years in 7U, or three years in 12U. Children with birthdays between January 1–July 31 will experience no changes. 


For more on this change, including info on requesting to play up a division, and players who just completed Grad Series, head over to the website.

Letter from the Commissioner 

In just the past month, it has happened. I’m not sure if it’s the start of daylight savings time, or one really hot day, or the choice that I made that I could wear shorts to the grocery store: Summer is coming. The sunshine and hot days will bring beach trips, air-conditioned movies, and vacations. 

For AYSO, the approaching summer means something else: registration time for the big fall season. Keep an eye out on our website and in your email for Early Registration so that you can reserve a spot for your family. We almost always end up with more kids signing up than we can find coaches and referees for their teams. So please seriously consider volunteering this year. There’s a place for everyone: coaches, referees, board members, photographers, uniform managers, and field setter-uppers. We need everyone’s help to make the Region go. 

Also, to get you in the mood for soccer, be sure to check out the LA Galaxy ticket offer above. I’d love to see you all at the game on May 2. It will be a great time!


—Patrick Shopbell, your volunteer RC
