Due to extreme heat in the forecast,
AYSO Region 13 activities are cancelled this weekend:
  • All scheduled games on Saturday, October 3, in all divisions are cancelled.

  • Saturday’s Picture Day will be rescheduled for a later date.

  • Sunday Soccer Lab, NEXT, and Upper Division games on Sunday, October 4, are cancelled.
While we are disappointed to lose a weekend of soccer, the health and safety of players is our top priority.

Please check ayso13.org for the latest updates on practices, games, and other activities for the coming week.
