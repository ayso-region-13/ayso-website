AYSO Region 13 families are invited to join Street Soccer USA and UNIQLO for the opening of the UNIQLO Street Soccer Park & Learning Center at St. Elizabeth Parish School in Altadena.

Created in response to the LA wildfires, this new community hub will provide free soccer programming, mentorship, life-skills workshops, and educational opportunities for youth and families. With two turf fields and a renovated learning center, the park is a place for play, healing, connection, and opportunity.
