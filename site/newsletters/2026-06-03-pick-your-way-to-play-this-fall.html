NEW Leagues for 16U & 19U
This fall, high schoolers can choose from two different programs, both offering fun soccer with friends and competitive play against teams from across Area 1C, including Glendale, Arcadia, Alhambra, and other local regions.

The Upper Division Recreational League is for players of all levels looking to practice and play in a well-structured soccer program built on the AYSO core philosophies. Every player makes a team, and rosters will be balanced with an equal distribution of skilled and newer players. This league is like the B16U “House League” we ran last year, but this season, teams from other regions will be part of the fun.

The Upper Division Competitive League is a program for players looking to train and compete alongside more advanced players. There will be one team formed per division, with a limited number of roster spots. This league will compete in the familiar upper-division program organized and run by Area 1C.

Women’s Coaching Alliance (WCA)—Applications Open!

Region 13 is proud to continue its partnership with the Women’s Coaching Alliance (WCA). The WCA’s program serves young women athletes in their formative years (ages 15-26) and builds their foundational leadership skills and confidence through coaching youth sports. Throughout her journey, a WCA coach is supported by a mentor, a community of peers, and an evidence-based curriculum, so she not only sees her potential to lead but also has the confidence to achieve it. Even better, by increasing female representation, a virtuous cycle begins, inspiring the next generation.

Learn to be a coach through the program with great training, practical application through coaching a team this fall, mentorship, and a stipend. 
Check out this flyer to learn more—applications are due June 12th!
Letter from the Commissioner

The World Cup is upon us! Starting on June 11, 48 teams from around the world will compete in stadiums across the US, Mexico, and Canada, culminating in the World Cup Final on July 19. I encourage everyone to watch a few games and cheer on their team. If you’d like to watch the US team play and enjoy this with others, come to the Pasadena Civic Center on June 12, or to our own Region 13 Clubhouse on June 25. It’ll be so much fun.

Many professional soccer players got their start in AYSO. This includes Cobi Jones, the famous midfielder, who first played soccer in nearby Region 4, and Alex Morgan, a striker on the World Cup-winning women’s team, who first played in nearby Region 31. Closer to home, Region 13’s own Brooklyn Posin now plays for Canada’s 16U Women’s National Team. Does this mean that parents should expect our players to become professional soccer phenoms? Most likely not. A good number of kids in our younger divisions won’t even remain in AYSO through their high school years, but our program fills an important niche as “Soccer for Everyone”. 

We give every child, regardless of their ability, finances, or family situation, the opportunity to play soccer. And I would encourage everyone to remember that we play a crucial role in that: we never know exactly what effect we will have on the children that are in our care. Maybe they will become the next Alex Morgan; maybe they will become the coach for our children’s children. Let’s help them develop into the best human beings they can be. And who knows—maybe they will excel at soccer too.

— Patrick Shopbell, your volunteer RC
