Spring League (10U- 19U)
Spring League is similar to the Fall core season, with balanced teams competing against each other. Games will be on either Saturdays or Sundays, with practices once a week. Spring League is open to both returning and new players. Depending on sign-up numbers, we may play against teams from neighboring regions, and girls and boys may play together. More details will be shared closer to the start of the program.

Spring Team (6U – 8U)
For younger players this Spring, there will be a weekly team meetup, consisting of training, followed by a scrimmage. If you are a certified coach and would like to help coach this activity, contact 6U-8U Spring Season Coordinator Neil Harrington.

Winter Stars Update


The Winter Stars Program with AYSO Region 13 has had an exciting and energetic start, bringing families together each Sunday at Marshall Fundamental for an afternoon focused on fun, development, and community. Over the first several weeks, players quickly settled into the flexible jamboree-style format, rotating between skill-building activities and small-sided scrimmage games while working alongside both Region 13 volunteer coaches and professional coaches. Families have embraced the once-a-week structure, which allows players of multiple age groups to participate at the same time and location, creating a convenient and welcoming environment for everyone involved.

In addition to on-field activities, the program has introduced several enhancements, including uniform shirt and soccer ball distributions, a dedicated Skills Area led by volunteer coaches from community partners, and increased family volunteer participation to help with setup, breakdown, and weekly operations. Players have shown growing confidence and teamwork each week, while parents have become more comfortable navigating the fields and engaging with the program. Winter Stars continues to emphasize a pressure-free atmosphere centered on learning, exploration, and enjoyment of the game, reinforcing AYSO Region 13’s commitment to providing a positive soccer experience for all families.
