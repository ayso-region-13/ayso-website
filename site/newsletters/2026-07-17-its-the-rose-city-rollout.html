Letter from the Commissioner
If you’re like me, as the World Cup winds down, you are asking yourself, “What am I going to do with my spare time now?” What a World Cup it has been! We cheered with strangers at watch parties, reveled in unexpected upsets, and commiserated with the fans whose teams didn’t go as far as they dreamed. I will always remember those Viking helmet-clad Norwegian fans, rowing together by the hundreds, pushing their team onward. It’s been an amazing six weeks of soccer.

Fortunately, soccer will continue in our little part of the world. Region 13 has over 2,000 kids ready to hit the fields this fall: that’s almost twice the number of players in the World Cup. And although we don’t actually need any Medical Trainers or Video Assistant Referees, we do need coaches, referees, team managers, and a slew of other volunteers to make our season go. 

Although it may seem like we spend more time asking you to volunteer than actually playing soccer, I feel that AYSO's volunteer nature 
is genuinely its greatest strength. We don’t just sit in front of a TV or in the stadium stands to enjoy AYSO soccer. We get out there with the kids, on the field, right in the middle of the action. AYSO lets us all become part of the game - the same beautiful game that the entire world has been watching for six weeks.

I look forward to us bringing that game to every one of our children and families in Region 13. It’s going to be a great season.

— Patrick Shopbell, your volunteer RC
