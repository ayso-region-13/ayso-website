Pitches in Peril: Fires in Los Angeles is a short documentary from Football For Future exploring how climate change is affecting football across the 2026 FIFA World Cup host nations. 

At its heart is the story of 16-year-old Nathan Bowser, an AYSO Region 13 alum from Pasadena, California, whose soccer community was deeply affected by the Eaton Fire. Through Nathan’s experience and his project replacing trophies lost in the wildfire, the film explores what football gives communities (from health and friendship to belonging and opportunity) and what can be lost when the places we play are disrupted. 

We’re proud of Nathan for sharing his story!

Letter from the Commissioner

Hello everyone,

AYSO is founded on the basic principle of Everyone Plays. For our Fall season, there are no tryouts and no restrictions on where you live or how much soccer experience you have. Region 13 offers tons of scholarships to try to reduce every obstacle to getting a child who wants to play soccer onto a team. The only thing “required” is that everyone pitches in as volunteers to make the season run.

When I walk around the fields on Saturday, I can see that philosophy in the cheers and laughs and smiles of everyone there. We are there because we want to be. The kids want to play soccer, and we have chosen to be part of a larger community that believes sports can bring people together. 

This weekend, that community includes families from Region 69 in the Palisades, and we are excited to welcome them. I believe Everyone Plays is what makes AYSO the amazing place it is, especially as we come together on the weekends. And that’s about my favorite part of Soccer Saturday. 

What’s your favorite part?

Patrick Shopbell
Your volunteer Regional Commissioner
