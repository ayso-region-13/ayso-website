Team and individual photos are taken during the fall season.

## Schedule

Picture days are held at Victory Park on scheduled Saturdays in October. This year those days are October 3, 17, and 31. Check your team's assigned time slot.

[Picture Day Schedule (Google Sheet)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1_WNahyjR7w2XZnHkRhA5NDtnmTB6XH8gNoAMeOHshXk/edit?usp=sharing)

## What to Expect

Teams are scheduled in time slots throughout the day. Players should:

- Wear their full uniform (jersey, shorts, socks)
- Arrive on time for their scheduled slot
- Bring their soccer ball

Photos include:

- Individual player photos
- Team photo

## Digital Photos

Free digital downloads of individual and team photos are emailed to families at the end of the season.

## Prints and Merchandise

Paid options are available for:

- Prints
- Plaques
- Mugs
- Keychains

Order forms are completed on picture day. Late orders can be placed through Nationwide Sports Photography Network.

## Makeup Photos

Makeup photo sessions are scheduled for players who miss their team's picture day. Dates are announced after the main picture days.
