# Cornishon Field

Cornishon is a smaller practice field in La Cañada, located near FIS Upper and FIS Lower.

## Location

[4400 Block of Cornishon Ave, La Cañada CA 91001](https://maps.google.com/?q=4400+Cornishon+Ave,+La+Canada,+CA+91001)

## Parking

Park on Cornishon Avenue. Do not use the Community Center parking lot — violators will be cited and towed.

## Restrooms

Restrooms are in the Community Center and are available during games and practices.

## Goal Setup

The lock for the goals is at the east side fence, to the left of the entrance. The combination will be sent to the head coaches of the first and last games of the day.
