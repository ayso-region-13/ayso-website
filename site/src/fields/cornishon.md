## Parking

Park on Cornishon Avenue. Do not use the Community Center parking lot — violators will be cited and towed.

## Restrooms

A restroom is in the building adjacent to FIS Upper and FIS Lower. (The Community Center bathrooms aren't available to AYSO.)

## Goal Setup

The lock for the goals is at the east side fence, to the left of the entrance. The combination will be sent to the head coaches of the first and last games of the day.
