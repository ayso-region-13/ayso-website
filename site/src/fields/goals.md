Teams with first games of the day provide volunteers to set up goals. Arrive 30 minutes before game time.

## Victory Park

### Equipment Location
Goals and equipment are stored in the container with the Region 13 logo in the parking lot. The field setup crew pre-sorts equipment starting at 7am.

### Goal Types by Age Group
- Fields 1, 2, 3 (10U): 2 goals each, 6 yards x 6 feet
- Field 4 (12U): 2 goals each, 7 yards x 7 feet
- Field 5 (6U): Two sets of 4' x 6' Bownets (4 total)
- Fields 6, 7, 8 (7U/8U): Two sets of 4' x 6' Bownets per field

### Setup Process
1. Goals are color-coded by age group (10U or 12U) with matching parts
2. Each kit includes hammer and stakes
3. 2-4 family members per team should assist with setup

### Teardown
Do not remove the net from the goal frame. Wrap the goal around the crossbar for disassembly.

### 6U/8U Bownets
Bownets for younger divisions are delivered by the field setup team.

Sign up for setup duty through WeJoinIn.

## Blair Upper

Goals are stored in silver storage bins, color-coded "10U."

## Pre-Assembled Goal Fields

At these fields, goals are pre-assembled and do not require setup:
- Blair Lower
- McKinley
- FIS (Flintridge Sacred Heart)
- LCHS-JV

**Important:** Lift goals when moving them. Do not drag goals across the grass. Corner flags are stored in designated bins.
