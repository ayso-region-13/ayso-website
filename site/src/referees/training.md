## Certification Requirements

### Step 1: Referee Instruction Course

Choose the course that matches your child's division:

**8U Official Course**
- 4-hour in-person class
- For refereeing 6U, 7U, and 8U games
- Minimum age: 10 years old

**Regional Referee Course**
- Full-day training (approximately 8 hours)
- For refereeing 10U and above
- Includes written exam (must score 75% or higher)
- Minimum age: 12 years old

Register through eTrainU.

[eTrainU](https://ayso13.inleague.com/eTrainu/index/)

### Step 2: LiveScan Fingerprinting

One-time requirement for all adult volunteers. Complete this at the designated location (Only Stop Travel on Woodbury Road). You only need to repeat this if you move to a new region.

### Step 3: Background Check

Complete annually through InLeague. The background check is free and processed by Sterling Volunteers.

### Step 4: Online Safety Courses

Complete these courses through eTrainU:
- SafeSport (annual renewal required)
- AYSO Safe Haven
- CDC Concussion Awareness
- Sudden Cardiac Arrest Awareness

## Training Schedule

Training classes are held before the fall season, typically in August. The region offers 2-3 Regional Referee classes at the Altadena clubhouse or in South Pasadena during July and August.

Refresher courses (2 hours, outdoor on soccer fields) are available but not mandatory.

[Training Calendar on eTrainU](https://ayso13.inleague.com/eTrainu/index/)

For training before July, contact [referee@ayso13.org](mailto:referee@ayso13.org).

## Badge Progression

After Regional Referee certification, you can advance through additional levels:

| Level | Requirements |
|-------|-------------|
| Intermediate | 25 games (5 in 12U minimum), 90% exam score, one observation |
| Advanced | 50 games (10 in 14U), 5 assistant games, fitness test, dual assessments |
| National | 100 games, 25 assistant games, fitness test, multiple assessments |

Each level increases the divisions you can referee and requires additional game experience and field assessments.

See [Referee Upgrades](/referees/upgrades) for the full pathway.

---

## Youth Referee Requirements

Youth referees (ages 10-17) complete the same courses with two exceptions:
- SafeSport training is not required
- Fingerprinting and background checks are not required

Each youth referee needs their own email address to track training.
