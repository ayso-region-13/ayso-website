question: Hello my question  is  if a  goalkeeper catches the ball then
  throws   or  puts  the  ball down can the striker take it and score the ball?
category: Goalkeeper Rules
---
Yes. Once the GK deliberately puts the ball down, it is a free ball and the GK cannot pick the ball up again and any player can come play the ball. 

But a couple of notes. When the goalkeeper tosses the ball in the air to punt, it is still considered to be in the goalkeeper's possession, and an opponent cannot challenge for the ball. Similarly, the goalkeeper may bounce the ball and catch (a basketball dribble), and no opponent can try to kick the ball away. (There really is no good reason for a goalkeeper to bounce the ball--once upon a time there rules were different and there was a good reason for the goalkeeper to do so. Most coaches will now teach that it's a bad idea to do because if the ball bounces funny and gets away from the goalkeeper, anyone can get it.)
