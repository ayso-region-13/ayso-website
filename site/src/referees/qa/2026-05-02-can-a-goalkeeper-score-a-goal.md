---
question: Can a goalkeeper score a goal?
category: Goalkeeper Rules
---
Yes, a goalkeeper is a player, like other players, and can score a goal. They can be responsible for an own goal, if they touch the ball and it goes into their own goal, or they could go to the other end of the pitch and score. They simply cannot use their hands outside of their own penalty area. (There is one other extremely unlikely scenario with goalkeepers. A goalkeepr is not allowed to throw the ball directly into the opposing goal--it would be a goal kick for the other team. But if you ever see that happen let me know!)
