## How Standings Work

Teams earn points through pool play:
- Win: 3 points
- Tie: 1 point
- Loss: 0 points

### Tiebreakers

When teams have equal points, ties are broken in this order:
1. Field points (wins, draws, losses)
2. Sportsmanship points
3. Head-to-head results
4. Goals allowed (fewest wins)
5. Coin toss

## Post-Season Competition

### Area Playoffs
Regular season pool champions represent Region 13 in the Area 1-C League Playoffs against other San Gabriel Valley regions.

### Rose City Cup
Standings determine tournament flights for the Rose City Cup, a single-elimination playoff held in December. See [Rose City Cup](/programs/tournaments/rose-city-cup).
