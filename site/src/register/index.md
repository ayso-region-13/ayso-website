## Pay What You Can

Region 13 offers registration on the honor system. Families who need assistance can pay what they can afford. Families who can contribute more help make this possible.

Adjust pricing at checkout or contact [registrar@ayso13.org](mailto:registrar@ayso13.org) for assistance.

## How to Register

1. Visit InLeague
2. Create an account or log in
3. Select your program (Fall, Spring, etc.)
4. Complete player information
5. Pay registration fee

[Register on InLeague](https://ayso13.inleague.com/app)

## Current Registration

Check InLeague for current registration status. Fall registration typically opens May 1 for the upcoming season.

Programs available:
- Fall Soccer (6U-14U)
- Upper Division (16U/19U)
- Preschool (4U/5U) — fall only
- Spring Soccer
- Winter Stars
- EPIC (inclusion program)

## What is Included

Registration fee covers:
- Uniform (jersey, shorts, socks)
- AYSO National Membership
- Insurance coverage

You provide:
- Soccer ball (see [Age Chart](/register/age-chart) for ball size)
- Shin guards
- Water bottle
- Cleats (optional but recommended)

A free used shoe exchange is held in August.

## Refund Policy

### Fall Soccer (6U-14U)
- Through July 15: Full refund minus $40
- Through Labor Day: Full refund minus $60
- After Labor Day: No refunds

### Upper Division (16U/19U)
- Through July 1: Full refund minus $40
- After July 10: No refunds

To request a withdrawal, complete the withdrawal form.
