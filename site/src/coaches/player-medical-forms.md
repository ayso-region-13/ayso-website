Each player completes a medical release form with important information during registration. Coaches **must** keep a hard copy of the medical release form with a fully certified coach at each practice or match.

We recommend placing your teams forms in a binder, with each form inside a sheet protector.

## How to Download

1. Login to [inLeague](https://ayso13.inleague.com/)
2. Click on "Rosters and Reports" under the "Teams" heading in the left menu bar
3. Use the selectors to find the correct team
    1. Select Season (e.g., Fall 2026)
    2. Program (e.g., "Core Soccer League")
    3. Division (e.g., "B14U")
    4. Select Teams (e.g., "B14U-04")
4. Select "E-Signed Medical Release Archive"
5. Then click "generate a merged release form" to get a single file for printing. You may also download individual forms by player name
