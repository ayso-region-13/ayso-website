## Before the Game

### Preparation

- Check your game time and field at [schedule.ayso13.org](https://schedule.ayso13.org/)
- Review your roster and know who's attending–follow up with families if needed
- If you need coverage for a coach being absent, reach out to your DCA (ideally a week before)
- If you need coverage for a referee being absent, first reach out to the opposing coach and see if their referees can cover your slot, if not then reach out to the DRA
- Bring completed game cards on cardstock (10U+)
- Bring equipment: medical releases, balls, first aid kit, pinnies
- Arrive 15-20 minutes early

### 6U-8U: Split Your Team

In 6U-8U, each team splits in half and plays on two fields simultaneously:

- Balance skill levels across both groups
- Assign one coach to each field
- After halftime, home teams switch fields

## During the Game

### 6U-8U Format

- No goalkeepers
- All players play the entire game
- Focus on getting players engaged: "get the ball and kick it"
- Repeatedly clarify which goal to attack vs. defend, and how to do restarts
- Use simple language with young players
- If teams are very unbalanced, at a water break of halftime, work with the other coach and mix your two teams up to balance them–use pinnies to clarify the new teams
- Don't track the score

### 10U-14U Format

- Manage substitutions to ensure equal playing time (in Region 13 Core, all players play at least 3/4 of every game, unless a team is overloaded on players, then all players play at leath half of the game
- Complete game cards on card stock
- Support referees — accept their calls

## Managing Young Players (6U-8U)

- Establish supportive team culture through team circles
- Use simplified language ("put your foot on it" rather than technical terms)
- Build peer connections to help hesitant players gain confidence
- Focus on participation over technique
- Celebrate effort and improvement

## Sideline Behavior

- Model good sportsmanship
- Don't go on the field unless the referee calls you on (Note: Parents should never go on the field)
- Speak respectfully to referees
- Keep families informed but focused on cheering, not coaching
- Remember: Referees ref, Coaches coach, Players play, Parents cheer

## After the Game

- Team huddle: celebrate the game, win or lose, keep talks brief
- Thank referees and opposing coaches and players
- Address any issues through proper channels

## Game Cards

For 10U+ divisions, bring completed game cards to each match:

- Pre-fill: team name, coaches, roster, location, date, time
- List players in jersey number order–list all players on the team. Tell the referee during check-in if any players are absent and if it is due to injury.
- Do no prefill positions/substitutions; things can change
- Referees use cards for tracking scores, infractions, and behavior
