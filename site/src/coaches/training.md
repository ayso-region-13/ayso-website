All coaches must complete training before the season begins. Training covers both safety requirements and soccer-specific skills for your age division.

---

## Required Training for All Coaches

### Administrative Requirements
1. Annual volunteer application (through InLeague)
2. Annual background check (through InLeague)
3. Livescan fingerprinting (once, unless you move to a new region)

### Online Safety Courses
Complete these through AYSOU/eTrainU:
1. SafeSport training
2. AYSO Safe Haven
3. CDC Concussion Awareness
4. Sudden Cardiac Arrest awareness

### Age-Specific Coach Certification
In-person or online course for your specific division.

---

## Certification by Age Division

| Division | Focus | Prerequisites |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| 6U | Run and kick toward the goal | None |
| 8U | Possession and passing skills | None |
| 10U | Territory control and positioning | None |
| 12U | Advanced tactics and positions | None |
| 14U (Intermediate) | Tactical development | One season as 12U coach |
| 16U-19U (Advanced) | Advanced coaching | One season as Intermediate coach |

## How to Enroll

### Online Courses
Register through AYSOU/eTrainU. Complete at your own pace before the season.

**[eTrainU](https://ayso13.inleague.com/app/eTrainU)**

### In-Person Classes
In-person coaching classes are offered August through September at various Southern California locations.

See [Training Classes](/volunteers/classes) for the current schedule.

---

## Certification Timeline

- Complete all online courses before the season starts
- Attend in-person class before your first practice
- Certification is valid for the season; some courses require annual renewal
