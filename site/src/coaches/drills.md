## Practice Structure

A typical practice includes:

1. Warm-up (10-15 minutes)
   - Light movement
   - Ball touches
   - Stretching

2. Skill Focus (20-30 minutes)
   - Age-appropriate activities from your manual
   - Small-group work
   - Individual skill development

3. Games (20-30 minutes)
   - Small-sided games
   - Scrimmages
   - Fun competitions

4. Cool-down (5 minutes)
   - Water break
   - Team circle
   - Preview of next practice/game

## Age-Appropriate Focus

### 6U-8U
- Basic ball control
- Running with the ball
- Kicking toward the goal
- Having fun

### 10U
- Passing and receiving
- Positioning
- Beginning tactical awareness

### 12U-14U
- Position-specific skills
- Team tactics
- Advanced techniques
