## Become a Sponsor

AYSO Region 13 serves over 2,500 players ages 4–19 in Pasadena, Altadena, La Cañada, and surrounding communities. Sponsorship helps keep registration affordable and funds equipment, field maintenance, and programs. All donations are tax-deductible — we are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
