# Volunteer FAQs

Common questions about volunteering with Region 13.

---

## Getting Started

### What do I need to do to volunteer?
1. Register as a volunteer through InLeague
2. Complete background check (free, through Sterling Volunteers)
3. Complete LiveScan fingerprinting (one-time, at Only Stop Travel on Woodbury)
4. Complete required safety training (Safe Haven, Concussion Awareness, Sudden Cardiac Arrest)
5. Complete SafeSport training (annual renewal)
6. Complete role-specific training (coach, referee, or team manager)

### How long does the background check take?
Allow several days for processing by Sterling Volunteers.

### Do I need to renew anything annually?
Yes. SafeSport training requires annual renewal. Background checks are also completed annually.

### What if my SafeSport completion doesn't show in InLeague?
You can manually request status correction by submitting your certification documentation to the appropriate email.

---

## Training

### Where do I find training classes?
Register through eTrainU, accessible through your InLeague profile. In-person classes are scheduled in August before the fall season.

### How long are the training courses?
- Safe Haven: 20 minutes online
- 8U Coach/Referee: 4 hours
- Regional Referee: Full day (approximately 8 hours)
- Division-specific coach training: 3-4 hours

### Do I need to complete training before every season?
Most training is one-time. SafeSport requires annual renewal.

---

## Roles

### What roles are available?
Team-level roles: Coach, Assistant Coach, Referee, Team Manager

Board positions: Region Commissioner, Division Administrators, Coordinators, and more. See [Volunteer Roles](/volunteers/roles) for the complete list.

### Can I volunteer without coaching or refereeing?
Yes. Team managers handle logistics and communication. Field hosts staff the information tent. There are also board positions focused on administration, events, and communication.

### How much time does volunteering require?
Time varies by role:
- Team Manager: A few hours weekly during season
- Coach: 1-2 practices weekly plus Saturday games
- Referee: Game days
- Board positions: Varies from 2 hours monthly to 20+ hours during peak periods
