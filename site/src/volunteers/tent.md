## Locations

- Victory Park: Near the playground
- Rose Bowl Area H: Near the blockhouse

## Sign Up

Volunteer through WeJoinIn.

[Sign Up on WeJoinIn](https://www.wejoinin.com/AYSORegion13)

## Responsibilities

### Setup
- Retrieve tent, tables, and chairs from storage
- Prepare the document box with schedules and blank game cards

### During Games
- Staff the information station
- Assist visitors with questions
- Direct people to teams using field maps and schedules
- Collect game cards from referees

### Breakdown
- Put away all items by 3:30 PM after the final game

### Lost Children
The tent serves as a meeting point for lost children and their parents.

## Donut Reimbursement

Field hosts may purchase donuts and coffee for volunteers:
- 1 coffee traveler
- 1 dozen donuts

Scan your receipt for monthly reimbursement.
