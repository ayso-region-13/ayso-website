# Team Manager Training

All team managers must complete training before the season begins.

---

## Required Training

### 1. Team Manager Orientation
- Watch the orientation video
- Review the orientation presentation
- Complete the task list at the [Manager Overview](/managers) page
- Contact [team@ayso13.org](mailto:team@ayso13.org) with questions

### 2. Background Check
- Access your profile through InLeague
- Click "Begin Background Check"
- If unavailable, select "Team Manager" in Volunteer Preferences for board approval
- Allow time for processing by Sterling Volunteers

[your InLeague profile](https://ayso13.inleague.com/app/family-profile)

### 3. Safe Haven Online Training (20 minutes)
- Log into eTrainU through your InLeague profile
- Access Training Library, then Safe Haven, then View Courses
- Enroll in "AYSO's Safe Haven 2022"

[eTrainU](https://ayso13.inleague.com/eTrainu/index/)

### 4. Required Reading
- Team manager orientation materials
- Volunteer Slack channel guidelines
- [Team Manager Tasks](/managers/tasks) documentation

---

## Recommended Training

### Concussion and Sudden Cardiac Arrest Training
Team managers are frequently the first to observe signs of a head injury. California law requires this training for certain youth sports roles. Complete through eTrainU.
