## Getting Started

### What training do I need?
- Watch the orientation video and presentation
- Complete background check
- Complete Safe Haven training (20 minutes online)
- Review team manager task documentation

See [Manager Training](/managers/training) for the full process.

### What is the time commitment?
A few hours per week during the season for communication, coordination, and game-day logistics. The workload varies based on how much you delegate to other parents.

### Do I need soccer knowledge?
No. The team manager role focuses on organization and communication rather than soccer skills.

---

## Tasks

### Which tasks can I delegate?
Nearly all of them. Common delegations include:
- Snack schedule coordinator
- Picture day helper
- Goal setup volunteers
- Field inspection volunteers
- End-of-season party planner

### How do I set up goal posts?
See [Goal Setup](/fields/goals) for detailed instructions. At Victory Park, arrive 30 minutes before the first game. Equipment is color-coded by age group and stored in the container with the Region 13 logo.

### What communication platform should I use?
Spond is recommended if the coach does not have a preference. Other options include team email, text groups, or similar apps.

## Picture Day

### How do I coordinate picture day?
Contact [pictures@ayso13.org](mailto:pictures@ayso13.org) for the schedule and process. Communicate details to families at least two weeks in advance.

### What if a player misses picture day?
Makeup photo sessions are scheduled. The Picture Day Coordinator will provide dates.

## Forms and Paperwork

### What does each family acknowledge at registration?
- Concussion Information Sheet
- Sudden Cardiac Arrest Information Sheet
- The AYSO [Kids Zone](/parents/pledge/) — ten standards for behavior at games and events

### How do I upload team photos?
Use the team-specific link provided by the organization.
