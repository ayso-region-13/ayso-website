A free soccer clinic for kids ages 6 to 9, led by coaches from the Angel City FC Coaching Network and hosted with the [Women's Coaching Alliance](/coaches/wca/).

[Sign up for the clinic](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe2v6Br5keO7CUcGZSEqvpUx3cL64eSRHsKWDI2v80ON1Eaag/viewform)

## When and Where


|  |  |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Date | Sunday, November 8 |
| Time | *9:30am - 11:00am* |
| Location | [Octavia Butler School](/fields/butler/), 1505 N Marengo Ave, Pasadena |
| Who | Co-ed, ages 6-9 (8U and 10U players) |
| Cost | Free |


## About the Clinic

Coaches from the Angel City FC Coaching Network will run the session. It's open to every child in the age range, whether they play on a Region 13 team or have never kicked a ball before.

Kids will work on their skills, build confidence, and spend a Sunday morning playing soccer with other kids their age. Please sign up ahead of time.

[Sign up for the clinic](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe2v6Br5keO7CUcGZSEqvpUx3cL64eSRHsKWDI2v80ON1Eaag/viewform)
