Board meetings are held on the first Thursday of every month at 7:15pm. Everyone is welcome to attend.

## Current Season

Current-season minutes are posted as they're approved. Minutes from prior seasons (2014–2022) are preserved in the project archive but no longer published here directly — request a specific year by emailing [rc@ayso13.org](mailto:rc@ayso13.org).

## Attend a Meeting

To join an upcoming meeting in person or virtually, see the [Region 13 Calendar](/about/calendar/) or contact [rc@ayso13.org](mailto:rc@ayso13.org).

## Contact

Questions about board meetings or to request specific archived minutes: [rc@ayso13.org](mailto:rc@ayso13.org).
