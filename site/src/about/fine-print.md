## Kids Zone

Region 13 follows the AYSO Kids Zone — a national program that sets ten standards for behavior at every game and event:

- Kids come first
- Fun matters more than winning
- Players play, fans cheer, coaches coach
- Respect for the volunteer referees
- Positive language at all times
- No weapons, alcohol, tobacco, or drugs at AYSO events
- Leave no trash behind
- Celebrate the player — win or lose

See the full [Kids Zone](/parents/pledge/).

## Health and Safety Disclosures

California state law requires that parents receive information on:
- Sudden cardiac arrest
- Concussions
- Prescription opioids

These documents are provided during registration.

## Privacy Policy

### Information Collected
- Name, date of birth, address, contact information
- Medical conditions relevant to participation
- Emergency contact information

### Information Not Stored
Credit and debit card information is not stored by AYSO.

### Background Checks
Background check data for volunteers is encrypted and restricted to Risk Management personnel.

### Photos and Videos
Photo and video permissions are requested during registration. Player identities are protected in public use of images.

### Sponsor Access
Sponsors do not have access to membership data.

## Disclaimer and Assumption of Risk

Participants acknowledge that soccer involves physical activity with inherent risks including but not limited to:
- Bruises, scrapes, and abrasions
- Strained, sprained, or torn muscles
- Broken bones
- Concussion and head injury
- Heat-related illness
- Other serious injuries

Participants and guardians release AYSO, facilities, sponsors, and volunteers from liability claims.
