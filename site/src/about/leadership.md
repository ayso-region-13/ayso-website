## Division Coordinators

The region has coordinators for each age division (4U through 19U), tournament direction, winter programming, and advanced play initiatives. See the [Coaches](/coaches/) page for more.

## Board Meetings

Board meetings are held on the first Thursday of every month at 7:15pm. Everyone is welcome to attend.

For meeting location and agenda, contact [rc@ayso13.org](mailto:rc@ayso13.org).
