When you register, your child receives:

- Jersey (team color assigned)

Need a replacement? Email [equipment@ayso13.org](mailto:equipment@ayso13.org) with a photo of the damaged item. Replacements are available on game days at the equipment tent.

## What You Provide

### Shoes

Sneakers work fine for soccer, especially for younger players.

If you want to buy soccer-specific shoes (cleats):

| Type | Best For | How to Identify |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| Sneakers/trainers | Any field, beginners | Flat rubber sole |
| Firm ground (FG) cleats | Most AYSO fields | Molded plastic studs |
| Turf shoes | Artificial turf only | Small rubber nubs instead of studs |

Firm ground (FG) cleats work on all our fields.

#### Sizing Tips

- Kids' feet grow fast — buy close to when shoes are needed
- Soccer shoes should fit snugly (tighter than regular shoes)
- Women's/girls' sizes: Add 1.5 to convert to men's sizing (Women's 8 = Men's 6.5)
- IDA brand makes cleats designed for wider feet

### Shin Guards

Shin guards are required for all practices and games. They protect the front of the lower leg from kicks and collisions.

For beginners, look for shin guards with an ankle strap or sleeve — these stay in place better than slip-in styles.

What to look for:
- Should cover from just below the knee to above the ankle
- Snug fit that stays in place
- Comfortable enough to wear for an hour

Shin guards are available at most sporting goods stores for $10-20.

### Soccer Ball (Optional)

Teams share balls at practice and games, so a personal ball is optional. If your child wants to practice at home:

| Division | Ball Size |
|----------|-----------|
| 4U-8U | Size 3 (smallest) |
| 10U-12U | Size 4 |
| 14U and up | Size 5 (adult) |

## What's Not Allowed

For safety, the following are not permitted during games or practices:

- Jewelry — including earrings, even if taped. Remove all jewelry before playing.
- Hard hair accessories — Soft fabric hair ties are fine. Hard clips, beads (except close-to-scalp braids), and headbands with hard parts are not allowed.
- Watches and fitness trackers — Must be removed or covered with a sweatband.

Exception: Medical alert bracelets may be worn but should be taped down or covered.

## Uniform Rules

### For Players

- Wear the full uniform (jersey, shorts, socks) to all games
- Shin guards must be worn under the socks
- No pockets on shorts (they can catch fingers) — plain black shorts are an acceptable substitute
- Undershirts, if worn, must match the jersey color

### For Cold or Wet Weather

- Long-sleeved undershirts: OK if they match the jersey color
- Sweatpants or leggings: OK under shorts for practice; ask your coach about games
- Jackets: Remove for games, OK for practice

## Uniforms for Coaches
