The Thanksgiving Tournament is a three-day competition held the weekend after Thanksgiving for 10U, 12U, and 14U teams.

![Thanksgiving Tournament game action](/images/thanksgiving-interior.jpg)

## Quick Facts

- When: Friday through Sunday following Thanksgiving
- Ages: 10U, 12U, 14U
- Location: Rose Bowl Area H (primary), Victory Park, and other locations in Pasadena
- Format: Three guaranteed pool games plus playoff rounds
- Open to: All interested teams (not limited to Region 13)

## Divisions and Fees (2025)


| Division | Format | Max Players | Registration Fee | Referee Deposit |
| -------- | ------ | ----------- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| 10U | 7v7 | 11 | $450 | $300 |
| 12U | 9v9 | 14 | $475 | $300 |
| 14U | 11v11 | 16 | $500 | $300 |


## Schedule (2026)

- Dates: November 27-29, 2026
- Registration should open in mid-October
- Location: Victory Park and other locations for 14U

## Registration

- Registration deadline: approximately two weeks before Thanksgiving
- Teams must have an AYSO ID to register
- Each team must provide a three-person referee team
- Registration handled through InLeague



## Referee Requirements

Each team must provide referees for assigned games. The $300 referee deposit is returned when referee obligations are fulfilled.

## Volunteer Opportunities

The tournament needs volunteers for:

- Field setup
- Check-in
- Scorekeeping
- General assistance

Sign up through the tournament portal.
