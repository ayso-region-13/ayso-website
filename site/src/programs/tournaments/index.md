Region 13 hosts two competitive tournaments each year for 10U-14U teams.

![Tournament game action at Region 13](/images/game-03.jpg)

---

## Our Tournaments

### Thanksgiving Tournament (Fall)
- When: Thanksgiving weekend (Friday-Sunday)
- Ages: 10U, 12U, 14U
- Location: Rose Bowl Area H and surrounding fields
- Open to teams from any region

[Learn more about Thanksgiving Tournament](/programs/tournaments/thanksgiving)

### Rose City Cup (Fall)
- When: First weekend of December
- Ages: 10U, 12U, 14U
- Location: Rose Bowl Area H
- For Region 13 fall teams

[Learn more about Rose City Cup](/programs/tournaments/rose-city-cup)

### Bill Owen Spring Classic (Spring)
- When: April
- Details announced early each year

## Playing in Tournaments

### How to Form a Tournament Team
For information on forming tournament teams or trying out for All-Stars, see [Tournament Teams](/coaches/tournament-teams).

### Requirements
- Players must be registered AYSO members
- Most tournaments require minimum game participation in regular season
- Teams need certified coaches and referees

---

## AYSO Tournaments Beyond Region 13

AYSO hosts tournaments at Section, Area, and National levels. For information about tournaments outside our region, visit [AYSO National Tournaments](https://ayso.org/ayso-tournaments/).
