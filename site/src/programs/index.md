## Choosing the Right Program

### New to Soccer?

Start with [Fall Soccer](/programs/fall-soccer) (ages 6-14) or [Preschool](/programs/preschool) (ages 4-5). These programs focus on learning the basics in a supportive environment.

### Looking for More Challenge?

- [NEXT](/programs/next) offers advanced training alongside your regular Fall Soccer team
- [All-Stars](/programs/all-stars) provides competitive tournament play in winter
- [EXTRA™](/programs/extra) Tryout-based select program. Fall through Spring.

### High School Age?

[Upper Division](/programs/upper-division) serves players ages 15-19 with a schedule designed to avoid conflicts with high school soccer.

### Need Accommodations?

[EPIC](/programs/epic) pairs players who need field assistance with volunteer buddies for a customized experience.
