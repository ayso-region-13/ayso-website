# Website Builder Comparison for AYSO Region 13

## Requirements
- Easy to use / WYSIWYG editor
- Easy for volunteers to edit
- Unlimited contributors
- Familiar with Squarespace

---

## Summary Comparison

| Platform | Contributors | Pricing | Ease of Use | Best For |
|----------|--------------|---------|-------------|----------|
| **Squarespace (Core+)** | Unlimited | $27-49/mo | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Recommended |
| **Wix** | 5-100 (plan dependent) | $17-159/mo | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Easiest overall |
| **Webflow** | Paid per seat ($15-39/seat) | $$$+ | ⭐⭐ | Overkill |
| **Google Sites** | Unlimited (free) | Free | ⭐⭐⭐ | Budget option |

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### 1. Squarespace (Recommended) ✅

**Contributor Limits:**
- Personal/Basic: **2 contributors only** (including owner)
- **Core, Plus, Advanced: Unlimited contributors**

**Pricing:**
- Core: ~$27/month (billed annually)
- Plus: ~$40/month
- Advanced: ~$49/month

**Pros:**
- You already know it
- Beautiful templates (38 in nonprofit category)
- Unlimited contributors on Core+ plans
- Built-in forms, galleries, blog
- Good mobile editing
- Member areas for volunteer-only content (optional)

**Cons:**
- Must be on Core or higher for unlimited editors
- No nonprofit discount advertised
- Locked to platform (no code export)

**Verdict:** Best choice. Core plan (~$27/mo) gives unlimited contributors and all needed features.

**Sources:**
- [Squarespace Permissions Explained](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206537297-Squarespace-permissions-explained)
- [Contributors Basics](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/115010355107-Contributors-basics)
- [Squarespace Pricing 2025](https://www.aboundwebdesign.com/blog-base/new-squarespace-pricing-plans-2025)

### 2. Wix

**Contributor Limits:**
| Plan | Collaborators | Price |
|------|---------------|-------|
| Light | 2 | $17/mo |
| Core | 5 | $29/mo |
| Business | 10 | $36/mo |
| Business Elite | 100 | $159/mo |

**Pros:**
- Easiest drag-and-drop editor
- 70% nonprofit discount in some countries
- Good volunteer management integrations
- Wix Studio (agency version) gives 5 free seats
- Online Programs feature for volunteer training

**Cons:**
- **Not unlimited contributors** - caps at 100 even on highest plan
- With 10+ volunteers editing, need Business ($36/mo) or higher
- Interface can feel cluttered
- Harder to maintain consistent design

**Verdict:** Great if you have <10 editors. Contributor limits could be problematic for volunteer org with board turnover.

**Sources:**
- [Wix Collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site)
- [Wix Studio Pricing](https://www.wix.com/studio/plans)
- [Best Website Builders for Nonprofits](https://www.tooltester.com/en/blog/best-website-builder-for-nonprofit/)

### 3. Webflow

**Contributor Limits:**
- CMS plan: 3 editors
- Business: 10 editors
- Additional seats: **$15-39/month per person**

**Pricing:**
- Basic: $14/mo (no CMS)
- CMS: $23/mo + $15/editor
- Business: $39/mo + $15/editor

**Pros:**
- Most powerful/flexible
- Professional results
- Code export option (not locked in)
- Advanced SEO tools

**Cons:**
- **Steep learning curve** - requires understanding of HTML/CSS concepts
- Per-seat pricing gets expensive fast
- Overkill for a volunteer soccer league
- No announced nonprofit discount
- Legacy editor seats being removed end of 2025

**Verdict:** Not recommended. Too complex and expensive for volunteer editors.

**Sources:**
- [Webflow Pricing](https://webflow.com/pricing)
- [Webflow Cost Breakdown](https://www.vezadigital.com/post/webflow-pricing)
- [Webflow Pricing Guide 2025](https://www.netguru.com/blog/webflow-cost)

---

### 4. Google Sites (Budget Option)

**Contributor Limits:** Unlimited (standard Google sharing)

**Pricing:** **Free** with Google for Nonprofits

**Pros:**
- Completely free for registered nonprofits
- Unlimited editors via Google account sharing
- Integrates with Google Drive, Calendar, Forms
- Zero learning curve if you use Google already
- Part of Google Workspace for Nonprofits (free)

**Cons:**
- Very basic templates - looks "free"
- Limited design customization
- No built-in donation tools
- Not as polished for public-facing sites
- Less professional appearance

**Verdict:** Good backup if budget is critical, but probably too basic for public-facing league site.

**Sources:**
- [Google for Nonprofits](https://support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/1614581)
- [Google Sites for Nonprofits](https://tamibenus.com/nonprofit-101/setting-up-a-free-website-using-google-sites-nonprofit-101/)
- [Google Workspace for Nonprofits](https://support.google.com/a/answer/2858465)

## Recommendation

### **Squarespace Core ($27/month / ~$324/year)**

Reasons:
1. **Unlimited contributors** - whole volunteer team can edit
2. **You already know it** - no learning curve
3. **Easy for volunteers** - intuitive WYSIWYG editor
4. **Professional look** - great nonprofit templates
5. **All-in-one** - forms, gallery, blog, calendar embeds
6. **Reasonable cost** - comparable to other options

### Alternative: Wix Business ($36/month)
Consider if you want the absolute easiest editor and know you'll have <10 regular editors.

## Additional Considerations

### Nonprofit-Specific Builders
These were mentioned in research but are likely overkill for AYSO:
- **Morweb** - nonprofit-specific, includes fundraising tools
- **Wired Impact** - built for nonprofits with volunteer management
- **Bloomerang** - donor management focus

### Features to Use in Squarespace
- **Blog** - for news/announcements
- **Gallery pages** - for photos
- **Form blocks** - for contact/feedback
- **Code injection** - for Google Calendar embed
- **URL Mapping** - for redirects from old site
